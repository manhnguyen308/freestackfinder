## Different tasks need different assistants

All of these services handle everyday chat, so the reason to use one instead of ChatGPT has to come from somewhere else: a long document handled in one pass, a research answer with sources, or an assistant inside apps you already use. Writing, coding, web search, file uploads, privacy, and app connections matter more here than model names.

Several free services cover work that would otherwise require ChatGPT Plus. **Claude Free** suits long documents, **Microsoft Copilot** adds web-grounded answers inside Microsoft apps, and **Google Gemini** connects with Google services. **Perplexity AI** centers its answers on cited web sources. **Meta AI** handles short questions inside WhatsApp and Instagram. The [free AI writing tools guide](/productivity/free-ai-writing-tools/) compares drafting and editing features separately.

Plan limits and model access change often. Before building a workflow around any free assistant, check the current free-plan limits for message volume, web access, file upload support, coding help, image tools, and privacy controls.


## Free assistants differ most at tools and context

OpenAI's [Free Tier FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9275245-chatgpt-free-tier-faq) lists web search, file uploads, data analysis, image generation, voice, and access to existing GPTs. Everyday text chats are currently unlimited subject to abuse safeguards, while tools have separate limits. An alternative is useful when one of those tool limits interrupts the job or another service fits the surrounding apps better.

No single tool is best for every use case. Pairing two free tools, one for research and one for drafting, costs nothing and covers more than either does alone.

People supplement ChatGPT when they want citations attached to search results, connections to Google or Microsoft services, a different document workflow, or access after a file, image, voice, or research limit has been reached.


## Five free ChatGPT alternatives

### 1. Claude free (Anthropic)

Claude is the one to try when a task comes with conditions and exceptions that have to stay consistent across a long document.

Claude's free access includes:
- Access to Claude on web, mobile, and desktop
- Long-context conversations for working with documents and drafts, subject to current free-plan limits
- File upload for document editing, summarising, and Q&A
- Strong instruction-following for tone, format, and length
- Web access on the free plan

The paid boundary is higher usage and persistent workspaces:
- Usage limits: Claude free throttles after sustained use, and limits vary by demand, conversation length, attachments, and model choice
- Free accounts can create up to five projects; paid plans allow more projects
- More model choice, higher usage, and priority access are paid-plan advantages

Heavy document work can use the variable session allowance quickly. Anthropic documents the [five-project free limit](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9519177-how-can-i-create-and-manage-projects).

[Use Claude free →](https://claude.ai)


### 2. Microsoft Copilot

Microsoft offers Copilot on the web, in Edge, and through Microsoft apps without a separate subscription:
- Free web and app access for general questions, writing, brainstorming, summarising, and image creation
- Web-grounded answers for current information
- Integration with Edge and Microsoft surfaces
- Available on iOS, Android, and web

Subscriptions add deeper Microsoft 365 access:
- Paid Microsoft 365 and Copilot plans add deeper Office app integration
- Free usage is subject to Microsoft's current credits, limits, and peak-time access rules
- Preferred access to the most advanced models during peak times is a paid-plan advantage

Copilot fits people who already use Edge, Windows, Outlook, or Office web apps. Web-grounded answers are available in the free service, while deeper document, spreadsheet, and inbox integration belongs to eligible paid Microsoft plans.

[Use Microsoft Copilot free →](https://copilot.microsoft.com)


### 3. Google Gemini

What Gemini offers a free account depends on the account and region, but it can include:
- General access to Gemini's fast model for everyday prompts
- Access to Google's current Gemini models at standard free limits
- Connections to Search, Gmail, Docs, Android, and other Google services depending on account and region
- Google Search integration for research-backed responses
- Image understanding and generation with current free-plan limits
- Access across Android (default assistant) and web

Google's paid plans raise the service limits:
- Google AI paid plans raise limits and add broader model and Workspace access
- Deeper Workspace features may require Google AI or Workspace subscriptions
- Daily free limits apply and can change without notice

Gemini fits accounts that already use Gmail, Docs, Drive, and Android. Connections and in-app features depend on the account, region, and subscription. Google's [Gemini limits page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en) is the current reference for model, context, and feature access.

[Use Google Gemini free →](https://gemini.google.com)


### 4. Perplexity AI

Perplexity attaches source links to every answer by combining generated text with real-time web search.

Perplexity's free search service provides:
- Basic searches without a published monthly quota
- Source citations with every answer: links back to original pages
- Follow-up questions within the same search thread
- File upload for document Q&A (limited on free plan)
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android

Advanced searches and model choice are limited:
- A free account currently includes five Pro Searches and three file uploads per day
- Paid plans add broader model choice and higher search and upload allowances

Perplexity fits research that needs links back to source pages. Its citations make verification possible, but they do not guarantee that a generated summary represents the source correctly. Open the cited pages before using a claim, and check the [current account tiers](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10352998-account-management-and-security) for its daily allowances. Our [free note-taking apps guide](/productivity/free-note-taking-apps/) covers places to organize the resulting notes.

[Use Perplexity AI free →](https://perplexity.ai)


### 5. Meta AI

Meta AI's advantage is placement rather than depth. It is built into Meta's web, mobile, and social apps.

Meta provides access through these surfaces:
- Access without a separate subscription or credit card
- Available inside WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Facebook, and on the web at meta.ai
- Image generation via Meta's Imagine tool
- Current-information and recommendation features, with availability varying by region and app
- Voice conversation mode on mobile apps

Availability and file support vary:
- File and document features vary by app and region
- Less suited to extended structured tasks compared to Claude or Copilot
- Weaker performance on complex reasoning and technical tasks compared to frontier models

Meta AI fits short questions and drafts inside supported Meta apps. It is a weaker fit for document-heavy work because file features vary across apps and regions.

[Use Meta AI free →](https://meta.ai)

## When to pay for ChatGPT

ChatGPT Plus makes sense when the separate file upload, data analysis, image, voice, or research limits interrupt regular work, or when a paid-only feature is required. Check [OpenAI's current plan page](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) before subscribing because plan names, model access, and limits change.

If your primary use is writing and editing, evaluate paid options against the tools in our [free AI writing tools guide](/productivity/free-ai-writing-tools/) before committing to a plan. Many writing-specific tools have generous free tiers that extend your effective usage without paying for a general-purpose chatbot upgrade.


## Research, drafting, and platform fit

A practical free setup is ChatGPT plus one alternative chosen for the limit you hit most often. Long documents point to **Claude**, answers you need to verify point to **Perplexity**, and files that live in Google or Microsoft accounts point to **Gemini** or **Copilot**.
